About me

About me

Hello! My name is Ivan Tolmachev, and I’m a Product Designer with over 15 years of experience in creating and scaling digital products in fintech, SaaS, and aerospace industries.

I create user-friendly, accessible, meaningful products that become a natural part of people’s lives. My work spans everything from hands-on design work to leading design teams, building design systems, and shaping strategy.

My specialty, though, is working with small businesses looking to grow customer retention, satisfaction, and loyalty rapidly. I believe design is about connecting business ideas with human needs. Check out some of my work below to see how connecting the two drove real value for my clients.
